Made for the conversation.

Your connection has its ups and downs.
Keep your people close.

FamCall focuses on one thing: a voice call with someone who matters. Audio first, a simple contact list, and connection recovery built into the call.

An internet connection is required on both phones. Performance depends on the network; no app can guarantee a call through every outage.

No passwords to remember. No meeting links to send. Just you and your people.

  1. 01

    Verify your number once.

    A text message gets you started. Then you stay signed in.

  2. 02

    Bring your people along.

    Add a number with its country code, or choose someone from your contacts. You both need FamCall.

  3. 03

    Tap, ring, talk.

    One person calls. The other answers. Recent and missed calls stay easy to find.
