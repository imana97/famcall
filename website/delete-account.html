FamCall

Delete your FamCall account.

You can request deletion in the app or contact SilverGrid Technology LLC if you no longer have access to it.

In the app

  1. Open FamCall and go to Settings.
  2. Choose Delete account.
  3. Verify your phone number and confirm Delete permanently.

Deletion continues on our servers after confirmation. It removes your account, saved contacts, blocked numbers, device registrations, and associated FamCall call history.

Without the app

Email info@silvergridtech.com with the subject “FamCall account deletion.” Tell us the phone number on your account, including its country code. We will confirm ownership before deleting it. Never email a verification code or password.

Request account deletion ↗

What may remain

Ordinary operational logs have a 30-day retention setting. After account deletion completes, a completion record becomes eligible for automatic removal after 30 days. Cleanup is periodic, so removal may take longer. Mandatory audit logs and some provider records are retained for longer, as explained in our privacy notice. A name or number someone else saved in their own contacts remains under their control. We do not store call recordings.
