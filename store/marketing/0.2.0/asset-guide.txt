FAMCALL — LAUNCH ASSET GUIDE
Version 0.2.0 • English only • Prepared 8 November 2026

VOICE
Direct, warm, calm. Keep the focus on calling people you know when the internet
is imperfect. Prefer short sentences and ordinary language. Avoid audience-age
labels, geographic targeting, reliability guarantees or security claims that the
release evidence does not establish.

BRAND
Use the approved Signal bridge icon with its tilted cream handset and interrupted
sage signal arcs. Keep the existing forest-green, cream and sage palette. These
materials use the app's approved icon, not a new identity. The display artwork
uses Arial/Helvetica so it can be reproduced without a proprietary font download.

CAMPAIGN
A little signal. A familiar voice.
Simple voice calls, built with weak and unstable internet in mind.

PUBLICATION
The website and its Android APK are live. Public App Store/Google Play pages are
not yet announced. Use famcall.web.app as the CTA until those public pages exist.
Store drafts can be saved without submitting review. Review forms, privacy/Data
Safety declarations, reviewer access and release qualification remain separate.

FACTUAL CONTEXT
Both people need FamCall and internet access. Calls connect FamCall users rather
than ordinary phone lines. Conditions on both phones affect calling. Data charges
may apply. No emergency calling. The app offers English and Persian while store
listings and launch assets are English only.

REPRODUCTION
From the repository root: npm ci --prefix store/release/tools, then set CHROME_PATH
if necessary and run node store/release/render-assets.cjs. Replace raw captures
with current genuine app screens when UI changes. Do not paint over or generate
app UI. Validate with node scripts/prepare-store-drafts.mjs.
