Short product description
FamCall is a simple internet voice-calling app for iPhone and Android. It uses
phone numbers to help people connect, with a clear contact list, direct dialing,
recent and missed calls, blocking, and simple mute and speaker controls. English
and Persian are available in the app, with light and dark themes.

Important context for longer announcements
Both people need FamCall and internet access. Calls connect FamCall users, not
ordinary phone lines. Availability and performance depend on both connections
and phone settings. Mobile data charges may apply. No emergency calling.
