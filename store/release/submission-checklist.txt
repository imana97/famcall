Planning document • Checked 6 November 2026

CURRENT STATE
The approved UI is implemented. Native Android and iOS simulator builds and
local automated checks pass as detailed in verification-report.txt. Store copy
and screenshot captures remain drafts until the release candidate is validated.
No mobile release has been uploaded. This checklist tracks remaining submission
work; it is not evidence of approval.

1. PUBLISHER AND APP RECORDS
- Use the developer account belonging to SilverGrid Technologies LLC. Confirm
  its legal identity, enrollment, roles, agreements, and required business details.
- Apple record created: app 6831551843, SKU famcall-ios, version 0.1.0.
  Both platform identifiers are com.silvergridtech.famcall. Apple signing is
  configured; Play app creation still awaits publisher declarations.
- iPhone and Android phones are configured; iPad support is disabled. Verify
  generated minimum OS versions against the candidate build.
- Confirm pricing, audience, countries of distribution, ads, content rating, and
  any current declarations from the real product. Do not infer an age rating
  or children-directed audience from the word "Fam" or family photography.

2. PHONE AUTHENTICATION AND SERVICE READINESS
- Enable the chosen Firebase phone-verification configuration and appropriate
  region policy, with a verified provider-side maximum of 50 SMS sent per day
  across the project, including retries. Confirm the actual quota name, scope,
  reset timezone, and enforcement behavior. A client counter or budget alert is
  not a hard SMS cap. Do not launch real signup if the cap cannot be enforced.
- Register the actual production and testing app identities and signing
  fingerprints. For Google Play installs, distinguish the Play App Signing key
  from the upload key. Validate authentication from the internal test build.
- Configure native phone-auth app verification, App Check where supported, and
  API restrictions against the chosen Firebase product tier. Test legitimate
  installs and documented fallback paths before enforcement. App authenticity
  and user authentication are separate controls.
- Use release-scoped backend permissions, short-lived calling credentials, and
  safe logs. Confirm no debug auth bypass, permanent relay secrets, or admin
  credentials are shipped in the application.

3. INTERNAL TEST DISTRIBUTION — REQUESTED CHANNELS
Android: Google Play internal testing. Upload a signed Android App Bundle to the
internal track, configure the intended tester list, and verify the real opt-in
link on a tester's Google account. Internal testing supports up to 100 testers.
Do not use an unlisted APK download as the primary requested testing channel.

iPhone: TestFlight. Upload the signed build to App Store Connect, complete the
beta description, feedback email, What to Test, export information, and any
required beta review access. Internal testers must hold appropriate App Store
Connect roles. External testers may need Beta App Review approval before testing.
Do not promise an external invitation works before the build is approved.

Conditional Google gate: qualifying personal developer accounts created after
13 November 2023 must complete a closed test with at least 12 testers opted in
continuously for 14 days before applying for production access. Confirm account
type; do not assume internal testing satisfies that separate requirement.

4. LISTING AND ARTWORK
- Copy the reviewed en-US fields; retain the checked character limits.
- Confirm that the release candidate actually supports each described feature.
- Supply the 512 x 512 Google Play icon and 1024 x 500 feature graphic, respecting

5. PRIVACY, DELETION, AND DISCLOSURES
- Inventory data processed by the app, servers, Firebase, the call provider,
  notifications, crash diagnostics, and other SDKs. Determine storage, purpose,
  sharing, association with identity, retention, and deletion for each item.
- Prepare the app-specific privacy policy from that inventory. Cover actual
  collection and handling; a website-only privacy notice is insufficient.
- Complete Apple's App Privacy responses and Google Play's Data safety form
  accurately, including relevant third-party behavior. Internal-only Google Play
  apps are exempt from that form, but complete it before a track that requires it.
- Implement a discoverable in-app account-deletion flow. Provide an accessible
  external web route to request account/data deletion for Google Play. Explain
  any legitimate retained data and retention periods accurately. Deactivation
  alone is not deletion. Verify ownership without creating an unnecessary barrier.
- Review microphone and contacts usage explanations, permission timing, and
  notification behavior. Manual contact entry must work after permission denial.
- Complete the actual encryption export questionnaire, permission declarations,
  age/content ratings, required SDK privacy manifests, and any relevant current
  platform declarations. Do not guess exemption answers.

- Meet the agreed gates in e2e-test-plan.txt and archive the run evidence.
- Provide working reviewer access and two-endpoint calling instructions.
- Check all supplied URLs from a signed-out browser and a mobile connection.
- Inspect the store listing preview for truncation, crops, and mismatched assets.
- Confirm legal contact details, screenshots, metadata, and build refer to the

  tested build. Do not display live download badges before links work.
- Use the owner-protected GitHub public-release workflow for store submissions.
  Populate candidate.json with the tested source commit, build numbers and
  passing evidence references first. Resolve review feedback, and verify the
  public listing and installation after approval. No approval is guaranteed by
  satisfying this preparation checklist.
