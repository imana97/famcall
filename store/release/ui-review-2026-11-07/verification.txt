ANDROID
Android 16 / API 36 emulator; 1080x1920 pixels, 360x640 dp. Denied microphone

notifications/full-screen access did not block that attempt; the local backend
reached ringing, and ending the attempt recorded cancellation.

search no-match/clear, history, block/unblock and returning from Android's
full-screen settings passed. Light/dark appearance and text scales 2.0 and 3.0
were inspected. The tab-label clipping found during review was fixed and retested.
Final b6204c0 passed the 30-step normal capture flow. Incoming and missed history
rows include explicitly seeded fictional display fixtures, not real call proof.

IPHONE
iPhone SE (3rd generation), iOS 18.6, 750x1334, plus iPhone 17 Pro Max,
iOS 26.5, 1320x2868, and a medium Dynamic Island simulator at 1206x2622.
Phone signup used the local Auth emulator only.
The native numeric-keyboard Done control dismisses the keyboard. Setup appears
before calling and requests microphone access through the native prompt; it
correctly does not request ordinary iOS notification permission.
Denying the microphone still permits browsing and adding a contact. A subsequent
call attempt returned to setup without creating a server call. A 00-prefixed
manual contact was normalized. Re-enabling the microphone in native Settings
was reflected after iOS relaunched the app. Settings and setup displayed the
allowed state. Light/dark screens and the largest Dynamic Type size were
inspected on the small display. The setup helper now scrolls and its primary
action remains reachable; the tab bar grows and wraps labels at very large text.
Final b6204c0 large/medium signup, native permission grant and capture flows passed.
The local outgoing call after permission recovery was cancelled by iOS Simulator's
missing native in-call UI host, not by microphone preflight. Exact native/service
evidence is in ios-simulator-callkit.json. It is not a successful voice-call test.

CAPTURE AND HARNESS NOTES
Store drafts are captures of the running E2E app, with fictional identities.
Where required, the fully opaque PNG alpha channel was removed without changing
any pixel color, with equality verified. No app-interface content was edited.
Android SystemUI and iOS status-bar overrides show consistent time/status icons;
these are presentation settings, not measured connectivity or battery state.
The capture manifest binds final screenshot files to source, dimensions and hashes.
Native transition frames were excluded after visual review; captures were taken
again after the interface settled.
Early harness failures were corrected separately: iOS hideKeyboard missed the
numeric keyboard; an unquoted 00 phone literal was interpreted as a YAML number;
Maestro launchApp defaults altered simulator permission fixtures; an iOS Settings
row tap did not toggle its switch; and a recovery assertion expected the old
route after the OS relaunched the app. Those runs are not reported as app passes.

LIMITS AND NEXT GATE
No personal physical phones, real SMS or production services were used by these
UI checks. They do not qualify two-way audio, measured network impairment,
Bluetooth/earpiece routes, or locked/background/terminated push delivery.
The user's earlier successful real-device audio report remains separate evidence.
Before public submission, test the final store-signed builds with actual APNs/FCM
calls, answer/decline, two-way audio, interruption/recovery and stale cancellation.
Android Settings Force Stop remains an OS block until the user reopens the app;
iOS VoIP relaunch must be tested rather than declared impossible. Permissions,
sound/Focus settings and network availability must be respected.
Public candidate.json remains not-ready. No public-store release is asserted.
