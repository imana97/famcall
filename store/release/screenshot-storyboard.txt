FAMCALL — SCREENSHOT STORYBOARD
Design draft • 6 November 2026 • Five portrait panels per platform

CURRENT CAPTURES
Actual development-build screenshots are in store/assets/screenshots-draft.
Android: people, recents, settings and add-person screens.
iOS: people, recents, settings and add-person screens.
The capture manifest records simulator/OS/version/locale and limitations.
These are genuine UI captures with fictional contacts, not release screenshots.
Recapture the signed release candidate before submission. No incoming, connected-call or reconnection panel is included while those
validation gates remain open. An attempted Android dialing capture had already
ended and was excluded from store assets.

FUTURE RELEASE PANELS
The following storyboard is conditional on the corresponding behavior passing
on the release candidate. Omit unverified panels. Never substitute a generated
interface or a wireframe for an actual app capture.

VISUAL DIRECTION
Warm ivory, deep green, generous spacing, large readable headings, and one clear
benefit per panel. Keep the real app interface prominent. Use fictional contacts
and demo avatars. Do not expose real numbers, verification codes, personal
notifications, or call content. Do not add fabricated testimonials, rankings,
download counts, store badges, network test scores, or unsupported guarantees.
For Google Play exports, keep taglines within 20% of the composition and devote
most of the image to real UI. Do not reduce the interface to a tiny phone mockup.

01 — THE PEOPLE YOU CALL
Headline: Your people. One tap away.
Supporting line: A simple contact list for everyday conversations.
Capture: Actual contacts screen with a small set of fictional saved contacts and
the control that starts a voice call. Capture both platforms separately.
Proof required: Tapping the displayed contact starts the actual outgoing flow.

02 — A FAMILIAR VOICE
Headline: A little signal. A familiar voice.
Supporting line: Voice calling designed for imperfect connections.
Capture: Actual active call screen with the connected person's name, live call
duration, and implemented call controls. No simulated quality indicator.
Proof required: Two-way intelligible speech and measured weak-network behavior.

03 — RECOVERY
Headline: A pause. Then back to talking.
Supporting line: FamCall tries to reconnect when the connection drops.
Capture: Actual reconnecting state triggered by a controlled interruption, then
an optional inset of the actual recovered call. Use only if the final interface
has this behavior and the recovery tests pass.
Proof required: Recovery is automatic under the supported test conditions. Avoid
implying recovery during complete or indefinite loss of internet access.
Fallback if not implemented: Omit this panel. Do not illustrate a future feature
as though it exists in the current version.

04 — AN INCOMING CALL
Headline: Pick up a familiar voice.
Supporting line: Answer a call from someone who matters.
Capture: Actual incoming call UI from the app or native calling integration on
the specific platform. Keep native platform UI intact and plausible. No invented
lock-screen layout or cross-platform mix of controls.
Proof required: Ringing, answer, and decline work in the documented app states.

05 — ADD SOMEONE
Headline: Add a number. Start a conversation.
Supporting line: Use your contacts or enter a number with its country code.
Capture: Actual add-contact screen. Show generic fictional information or a
masked test number. Do not show an unowned working phone number.
Proof required: Permission-declined and manual-entry paths both work.

EXPORT TARGETS
Apple iPhone: 1320 x 2868 pixels portrait, opaque PNG, sRGB. This is one of the
accepted 6.9-inch screenshot dimensions checked on 6 November 2026. Five images
fit Apple's 1–10 screenshot range. If iPad support is enabled, add genuine iPad
captures at a currently accepted 13-inch dimension; do not stretch iPhone art.

Google Play phone: 1080 x 1920 pixels portrait, opaque 24-bit PNG or JPEG. Five
images fit the 2–8 phone screenshot range. Keep exported images within the
documented 320–3840 pixel bounds and the 2:1 maximum dimension relationship.

CHECKS BEFORE UPLOAD
Inspect at full resolution and at store thumbnail size. Read every line. Check
cropping, contrast, actual UI consistency, fictional data, and asset order.
Compare captures against the release candidate's build number. Record the device,
OS, build, locale, and capture date in the release evidence. No alpha for Apple
screenshots. No cross-platform store logos or unsupported availability badges.

OPTIONAL VIDEO
An app preview is not required for the initial listing. If produced later, use
real app footage and the current platform specifications. A suggested sequence
is contacts → tap to call → incoming call → conversation → hang up. Do not use
generated UI footage as evidence of the working product.
