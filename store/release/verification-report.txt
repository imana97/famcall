Measured median RTT across the two samples was 14.5–15 ms, 182.5–185 ms,

windows ranged from 0%, 0.76–1.09%, 2.76–3.37%, and 4.08–13.19% respectively.
The small windows include silent-direction DTX traffic. These are realized
measurements, not guaranteed values of the configured random process.

The stress scores show substantial degradation. STOI is not a percentage of
words understood, and no human intelligibility or supported-network claim is
made. These pilot profiles do not substitute for the N0–N6 release matrix.
The v2 shaper includes 28-byte IPv4/UDP headers in its bandwidth accounting;
link-layer and VPN overhead remain excluded.

TRANSPORT OUTAGE PILOT — PACKETS ONLY
One native Android call recovered received packets at both peers after each
5-, 15-, and 30-second 100% relay-drop interval. First observed recovery after
restoration: 3.020/3.092 s, 2.144/2.216 s, and 5.639/5.713 s respectively.
The lab counted 158, 637, and 830 dropped packets. The 30-second case included
a disconnected/failed transition and reconnection. Statistics arrive about
every three seconds; these are observation bounds, not precise recovery latency.
These preliminary outage runs used the earlier v1 shaper with no bandwidth cap.
They did not measure speech recovery and do not clear G4/G5.

with current statistics and advancing received packets at both peers. These

blocking or prove automatic fallback. The UDP shaper did not impair these paths.

The delayed UDP call below was then observed for eight minutes after it connected.

deadline and first connected about 160 seconds after answer. One peer lacked a
local relay candidate during the delay. The underlying allocation delay is not
diagnosed. It exposed an application retry gap: native ICE can remain new without
emitting disconnected/failed. Source now starts bounded initial retries after

MEASUREMENT RULES
The audio lab injects generated speech into the emulator microphone and captures
the receiving emulator output. It never uses the computer's microphone. STOI is
an intelligibility estimate, not a MOS score, human listening result, or proof of
physical-device performance. Tests check the actual selected ICE candidate path.
Per-leg impairment settings must not be reported as end-to-end loss/latency.
Only completed, reproducible cases with valid source/capture signals count.
