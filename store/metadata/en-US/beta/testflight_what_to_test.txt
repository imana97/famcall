FamCall 0.2.0

This update brings a simpler interface, direct dialing, English and Persian, and incoming-call fixes.

Please check:
• People: search, add, swipe to edit/remove, and update a saved phone number.
• Dial pad: the centered button opens a bottom sheet. Enter a number with + or 00 and its country code to call another FamCall user.
• Calls: receive and answer with the phone locked or FamCall in the background; check mute, speaker and hangup.
• Settings: switch between English and Persian. Both use the same left-to-right layout and your choice stays saved.
• Log out and sign back in without deleting your account.
• Account deletion: verification and clear confirmation before permanent deletion.

The other person also needs FamCall. Network and phone settings can affect delivery. FamCall does not support emergency calls.
