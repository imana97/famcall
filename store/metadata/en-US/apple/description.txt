A little signal. A familiar voice.

Some conversations are worth making time for, even when the internet has other plans. FamCall keeps voice calling simple, with weak and unstable connections in mind.

YOUR PEOPLE, ONE SIMPLE LIST
Add a name and phone number, or choose someone from your phone’s contacts. Tap a person to call. Only the contacts you choose are added.

JUST WANT TO DIAL?
Open the keypad, enter a number with its country code, and call another FamCall user. No need to save the number first.

LESS TO THINK ABOUT
Verify your phone number once and stay signed in. No password or meeting link to remember. Clear controls for mute, speaker and ending a call keep the conversation front and center.

KEEP UP WITH YOUR CALLS
See recent and missed calls. Edit or remove saved people, block an unwanted number, and unblock it whenever you choose.

FAMILIAR IN MORE WAYS
Choose English or Persian. Enjoy a clear light or dark appearance that follows your phone’s theme.

MADE WITH IMPERFECT CONNECTIONS IN MIND
FamCall focuses on voice. If a connection is interrupted, it tries to reconnect and shows you what is happening.

Both people need FamCall and an internet connection. Calls are between FamCall users, not ordinary telephone lines. Availability and performance depend on both connections and phone settings. Mobile data charges may apply. FamCall does not support emergency calls.
