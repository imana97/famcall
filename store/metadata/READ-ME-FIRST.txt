CONTENTS
metadata.json is the source of truth. en-US/apple and en-US/google-play contain
individual paste-ready text fields. Run python3 store/release/prepare_metadata.py
to export and validate them. validation.json records field limits and claim checks.

store/assets/public-0.2.0 contains six actual app-screen compositions per store,
the approved icons, Play feature graphic, raw captures, review preview and hashed
asset manifest. store/marketing/0.2.0 contains four launch images, the illustration
source and launch-copy.txt. Store assets use no fabricated app interfaces.

The captures show the current 0.2.0 app UI from the release source in isolated
native E2E builds using local demo fixtures. Names, 202-555-01xx phone numbers and
call history are fictional. They are not production-call or network-test evidence.
The AI-generated family photograph is an editorial illustration, not a testimonial.
The approved icon and forest-green, cream and sage palette are preserved.
